# Registration as an errand — a catalog of sites, accounts without descriptions

**Status:** design, 2026-11-03. **Replaces** the described-site sign-ins: site descriptions
(`adapters/<site>/account.yaml`) with their steps, their signature and upload, `/provision-site`,
recorded paths and their approval, replay, the signed-in check, keep-alive and its schedule. **Keeps**
the catalog, as a list of small site cards. Builds on errands (`2026-11-03-errands-design.md`), whose
behaviour it reuses unchanged.

## Why

A site the box can register on today needs a developer to provision it first: a signed description
with a recorded path per step, written on a Mac with `/provision-site`. Errands showed that the model,
on general rules and the owner's answers, finds its way through an unknown site's flow live, cheaply,
with the owner watching. The owner wants registration done exactly the same way: pick a site, the box
signs up on its own screen, asking the owner every choice; afterwards that account is signed in and
any errand on that site uses it. No provisioning, no steps, no paths.

The catalog stays (owner, 2026-11-03): "it's like pre-approved sites/apps. User can choose and
register from that, also he can able to add own any site just clicking 'add'" — and Add makes the
same small card ("when I click add - it adds small specs with name/icon/etc").

## Goals

- **A catalog of site cards.** A card is a site's name, one-line description, link and icon — nothing
  about how to register. Two sources:
  - **Pre-approved**: one file per site in the repo, `catalog/<name>.yaml` (`name`, `description`,
    `link`, `icon` as a small PNG data URL), shipped by the deploy; being on `main` is the approval.
    The box loads them at start. *(Call: no signing key or upload API — the deploy is the only way in.)*
  - **The owner's**: Add in the console takes any http(s) link; the box makes the card from the page
    itself — its title as the name, its meta description, its icon (the worker fetches the page, as
    `provision icon` did) — and keeps it. The owner's cards can be removed; pre-approved ones can't.
- **Register from a card.** Every card has Register (or its account's state). The run is an errand
  whose action is "create an account", starting at the card's link: the same worker
  module, model, providers, rules, questions (tap a choice or type), captcha hand-off, live view,
  recording, cost by provider, Stop, Full log, needs-you bar and run page as an errand.
- **The box's identity, filled without reaching the prompt.** The model fills the owner's details by
  NAME through the worker's `fill_field`: `email` (a fresh plus-address of the Email connector's
  mailbox, as today), `first_name` and `last_name` (the Account's names), `phone` (the box SIM's
  number, as today) and `secret:password` (generated by the engine, sealed in the account row). Any
  other detail the site wants (a birth date, a postal code, a gender) is a choice: the model asks the
  owner with `ask_user`.
- **Codes and links the box receives.** An SMS code to the box SIM and an email (code or link) to the
  attempt's plus-address are fetched by the engine through the existing listeners and entered by the
  worker; neither code nor link ever reaches the prompt, the run's log or browser-use's log.
- **No Confirm before it's created.** The owner's press of Register is the approval: the
  registration's prompt presses the control that finally creates the account without asking. Each
  kind has its own prompt (`agent.py`'s `PROMPTS`, picked by `run_agent`'s `kind`, contract v13–14);
  only an errand's asks "Confirm: …?" before its final press. (Owner, 2026-11-03: "Errands should ask
  for the final confirmation, while sign-ins and register should not".)
- **A site is keyed by its link's registrable domain** (eTLD+1, `golang.org/x/net/publicsuffix`,
  already a dependency): `booksy.com` for `pt.booksy.com` or `www.booksy.com` alike. A card, its
  account, its Chrome profile, runs and recordings share that key; one card per key (Add of a domain
  already in the catalog opens that card instead). An errand whose link has the same registrable
  domain runs in that account's profile. *(Builder-check call: host-without-www would miss a sign-up
  on a subdomain.)*
- **Only a `registered` account is an account.** A `warn` one ("the registration was interrupted")
  gives an errand no profile and no values (a throwaway, as a site with none), and Register on it
  starts over: new plus-address, new password, profile wiped. *(Call.)*
- **Signing in again happens inside the errand.** An errand on a site with an account gets
  `fill_field` with `email` and `secret:password`, and the SMS/email tools; when the site asks to sign
  in, the model signs in with them and goes on. Nothing checks or signs in on a schedule (owner,
  2026-11-03: "replay does not needed"; read here as: no background checks either — a lapsed session
  is found and fixed by the next errand).
- **Check sign-in, by the owner's press.** A registered account's page has **Check sign-in**: an
  errand of kind `check` at the card's link, in the account's profile with its email and password,
  whose action is "sign in, or see that the account is signed in already". It ends "signed in" or
  "not signed in" and changes nothing on the account; it is the site's latest status. Signing in is
  never a Confirm question (its prompt, and the account rules, say so). (Owner, 2026-11-03: "for the account I can
  check sign-in if it's registered"; "card should show latest status".) Still nothing on a schedule.
- **The box carries over.** The three described sites become pre-approved cards (`catalog/booksy.yaml`,
  `catalog/thefork.yaml`, `catalog/idealista.yaml`, from each description's name, description,
  `home_url` and icon). The two accounts move to their keys, `booksy` → `booksy.com`,
  `thefork_pt` → `thefork.pt`, keeping email, sealed password, owner names, profile, and their errand
  runs and recordings. The description-era runs (kinds `register`, `check`, `sign_in`) are deleted
  with their videos in the migration, as v13 dropped runs: their page (roadmap, points) is gone and
  they have no errand row. *(Call.)*

## Non-goals

- No description steps, signature, upload, versions, recorded path, path approval, replay, signed-in
  check, keep-alive tick, "Check" or "Try again" button, register proof, roadmap of points, password
  rules, owner fields typed at Register. All of it is deleted, with its tests, IPC messages
  (`run_flow`, `run_check`, `account_check`, `execute_step`, `session_check`), UI and docs (no
  backward compatibility, CLAUDE.md).
- No editing a card in the console (the owner removes and adds again).
- No sign-in for an account the box didn't create (the owner's own existing account elsewhere).
- No deleting the account on the site. Remove forgets it on the box (row and profile); its runs stay
  in the Errands list, and the journal keeps the address, as today.

## Anti-goals

- A password, code or emailed link in a prompt, a log line, a trace or a recording's log.
- Any site's, domain's or action's words in the rules besides the run's action (CLAUDE.md "Prompts are
  general"; `test_the_prompts_are_general` covers the new rules too). A card never reaches a prompt.
- A second way to do the same thing: registration is an errand, not a parallel flow.

## How it works

### Worker

`run_errand` takes, besides today's fields, `values` (field name → value; secret names prefixed
`secret:`) — empty for an errand on a site without an account. With values it registers `fill_field`
(`fill.py`, unchanged in principle: the model names a field and an element, the value is typed with
browser-use's sensitive typing and never echoed) and two waits, both answered by the engine through
`value_request`:

- `sms_code(index)` — asks the engine (`value_request` name `sms_code`) and types the code into the
  element. The engine takes the first SMS stored after the run started holding a 4–8 digit code, any
  sender, on the code wait's clock (today's `codeWait`, without a sender filter).
- `email(…)` — asks the engine (`email`) for the first email to the attempt's address after the run
  started, any sender (the plus-address is the attempt's own). The model is shown its subject and text
  with every link replaced by `[link N: <its words>]` and every 4–8 digit code by `[code N]`; it then
  calls `open_email_link(n)` (the worker opens it in the run's tab, at most once) or
  `enter_email_code(n, index)`. The real links and codes stay in the worker's closure.

The rules gain one general paragraph, present only when values are given: what `fill_field` offers
and that the owner's details are filled only through it; that a code or a link the site sends is
fetched with `sms_code` / `email`; and that a site asking to sign in is signed in with `email` and
`secret:password` (without values, the site asking to sign in stays a `human_check`). The action for a
registration is the run's own text, "create an account" (the task adds "for the owner") — the only
specific part.

A new request, `site_card {url}`, fetches the page over plain HTTPS (no browser, no model, a short
timeout, a size cap) and answers `{name, description, icon}`: the page's title, its meta description
(one line, cut to 200 characters) and its icon as a ≤16 KiB PNG data URL (the code `provision icon`
uses today, moved here); a missing piece is empty, never invented.

Policy gate, providers, models, screen size, flash mode and per-call `model_call` cost are errands'.

### Engine (`internal/signins`)

- **Catalog**: a `sites` table (key, name, description, link, icon, source `catalog` | `owner`,
  added_at). At start the engine loads `catalog/*.yaml` from `-catalog-dir` (the deploy ships
  `/opt/concierge/catalog`) and makes the `catalog` rows equal to them. `AddSite(ctx, link)`:
  http(s) link → key; an existing key answers that card; else `site_card` through the worker, a name
  defaulting to the key, stored as `owner`. `RemoveSite` removes an owner's card (refused while it
  has an account or a run).
- `Register(ctx, key)`: the card's link; refuses a key that already has a `registered` account; needs
  the Email connector's address, the box's phone and the Account's names (`NotReadyError` as today);
  claims the key, journals the attempt's plus-address, generates and seals a password (one recipe: 16
  characters, at least one lower, upper and digit, one `!`), writes the account row (`warn`,
  "the registration was interrupted"), and runs `run_errand` with kind `register`.
- Its outcome: the model's `success` → the account is `registered`; otherwise the row is deleted and
  the profile wiped (as a failed registration today); cut short by Stop or the engine stopping →
  `warn` "the registration was interrupted" and the profile kept, so the owner can register again.
- `StartErrand`: the account by key replaces `errandSite`'s description lookup; with a `registered`
  account the request carries `values` (email, password); no check before. The allowed domains are the
  key and its subdomains.
- Store v14: `sites` (above); `accounts` keyed by key, `state` `registered` or `warn`, its
  registration run; drop `versions`, `paths`, `run_points`, `checked_at`. Migrate the account rows,
  `runs.site` and `journal.site`, and move `sessions/<site>` and recordings to the key.
- IPC contract v10: `run_flow`, `run_check`, `account_check`, `execute_step`, `session_check` removed;
  `site_card` added; `run_errand` gains `values`; value_request names `sms_code` and `email`.

### Console

- Sidebar "Sign-ins" → **Accounts** (`/accounts`): the catalog as cards — icon, name, description,
  and the account's state (not registered, registering…, registered with the box's email for it, or
  the warning) with Register, a link to the registration run, and Remove (the account; an owner's
  card can also be removed once it has none). **Add** opens a link box; the new card appears in the
  list. A registration is an errand run of kind `register` with its errand row: it behaves exactly as an
  errand but lives on the Accounts pages — its site's page lists it (with the site's sign-ins: errands
  that filled the box's password), its page is `/accounts/{key}/runs/{run}` (the errand run view,
  back to the site), and the Errands list leaves it out. (Owner, 2026-11-03: "registration for the
  account should use the same behaviour but live in the UI/UX on the different pages".) A sign-in
  check (`POST /api/v1/accounts/{key}/check`) lives there the same way. Each card shows its latest
  status: the newest of its registrations, sign-in checks and sign-ins (`SiteView.latest`), what it
  was, when, and how it ended; while a run works on the site, what it is doing (checking sign-in…).
- Deleted: the site page with versions, learned paths, Check / Try again, the roadmap, the sign-in run
  page, the register dialog with owner fields.
- Needs-you bar: a registration's or a sign-in check's question or captcha, like an errand's.

### Repo

`catalog/` holds the pre-approved cards. Delete the sign-in parts of `adapters/` — `booksy/`,
`idealista_pt/`, `thefork_pt/`, `signing.pub`, `account.schema.json`; the graph adapters
(`europcar_pt`, `guerin`, `medis`, `multicare`, `manual`, used by `domains/`) stay — and
`account.py`, `provision.py` (its icon code moves to the worker's `site_card`), `replay.py`,
`session_llm.py`, the `provision-site` skill, and whatever in `step.py`, `trace.py`, `har.py`,
`recorder.py`, `outcomes.py`, `target.py` only the removed requests use. Update CLAUDE.md (the sign-in
bullets, the description-dependent ones: register proof, email link, roadmap, signed-in check,
provisioning) and the READMEs. "No code or prompt names a site" keeps its three guards
(`nosite_test.go`, `test_no_site_named.py`, `words.test.ts`): they read the names and hosts from
`catalog/*.yaml` instead of the descriptions — a site is data there, as it was in `adapters/`.
